Vane draws a site as a home page, docs in a tree of groups and pages, and news. It is named after the weather vane, which turns to show which way the wind blows: a docs site is there to show the way.

## What it draws

- **A home page** with a kite in the sky behind its headline, buttons and a command to copy, two windows of the product, features hung on a string, a band of pictures, the latest news and closing cards.
- **Docs** in the groups and the order you set, with the tree beside each page, the time it takes to read, a table of contents, and links to the previous and the next page.
- **News** from Kite's posts, with tags, categories and an RSS feed.
- **A search by title** across the docs tree, from any page. Press `/` to open it. With Kite's official search plugin, the same box searches the whole text.

## How it looks

Warm paper and ink by day, with a faint grain, and headings in a serif. At night the paper turns to a dark sky, and the kite on the home page glows. One accent color draws the kite, the seals that number the features, the links and the keyboard focus.

## What it asks of a reader

Nothing from a third party: no web fonts, and no scripts or styles from elsewhere. Headings use Noto Serif SC when the site loads it, and the serifs of the reader's system otherwise. Every page works without a script, and day and night follow the reader's system until they choose one.

## What it is not

Vane is not a blog theme; Kite's default theme is one. It does not keep several versions of the docs side by side, and on its own its search looks at titles only.

## Where it lives

Vane has its own repository and its own releases. Kite ships only its default theme, and a site installs Vane from a release, as [Installing a theme](/installing-a-theme/) describes.
