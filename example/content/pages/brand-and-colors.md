## Logo and title

`logo` is drawn in the header before the site title. An SVG, or a picture about 64 pixels tall, suits it best. Turn off `show_title` when the logo already spells the name.

## Site icon

`favicon` is shown in browser tabs and bookmarks. A square picture of at least 64 pixels suits it best.

## Accent color

`accent` colors the links, the current page in the docs tree and in the table of contents, and the buttons. The suggested colors all carry white text; on a dark page the theme lightens the accent by itself so that links stay readable.

| Color | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Kite blue | `#3d65bd` |
| Teal | `#0f766e` |
| Violet | `#6d4fc2` |
| Rust | `#b4530e` |

## Light or dark

`color_scheme` sets what a reader sees first: `auto` follows their system, and `light` or `dark` holds one. Either way the button in the header switches, and the reader's choice is kept in their browser.
